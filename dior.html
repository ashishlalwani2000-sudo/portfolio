What the work
looks like.

Before writing this board I read how Dior’s travel retail spaces have been built and reported. These are the formats an architect there can end up drawing.

The counter

Fragrance, beauty, skincare

In Bahrain, one three-in-one counter of 28 m² carried all three, with black and white branding the retailer called a beacon for travellers. In Auckland, a counter was built around visibility across the whole perfume range.

Maison Christian Dior

Boutiques inside airports

Heathrow Terminal 5 was the first Maison Christian Dior boutique in European travel retail, dedicated to the love of perfume. Paris Charles de Gaulle followed with its own.

The flagship

The largest in the world

At cdf Sanya, Dior opened its largest fragrance and beauty flagship worldwide, where clients can personalise pieces with cords, charms and engraved messages. This is the scale where the posting asks for a project manager.

Beyond the shop

A spa and a barber in an airport

Qatar’s Hamad Airport has the Dior Luxury Beauty Retreat: Dior’s largest spa and the first with a barber shop. Its suites are each inspired by a part of Christian Dior’s life, and one has a ceiling of stars.

Pop-ups and installations

Stories built inside a terminal

Changi’s Provençal summer getaway centres on a boat, the biggest of its kind Dior has made for travel retail. Incheon had the first Plan de Paris pop-up in Asia Pacific, and Hong Kong had Atelier of Dreams for the festive season.

Dior grey, 1946

The colour behind the counters

Christian Dior chose this grey for his boutique at 30 Avenue Montaigne, and the house still builds rooms around it.

Sources: The Moodie Davitt Report, Dior’s own job postings and Paris Select Book for the grey. Descriptions are as reported there, not from inside Dior.
